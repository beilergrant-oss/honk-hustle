Privacy Policy

Bus Blitz Party · Last updated: October 2026

Bus Blitz Party is a puzzle game. We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not run our own servers. Your levels, coins, settings and items are saved only on your device.

Ads

The game shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, your approximate location (from your IP address), and information about how you use the game and the ads. If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, we ask for your consent first, and you can change your choice any time in the game under Settings › Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. Learn how Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Buying “Remove ads” turns off the ads between levels. Reward videos you choose to watch still come from AdMob.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play (Android) or the App Store (iPhone). We never see your payment details. To confirm and restore purchases we use RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase receipts. See revenuecat.com/privacy.

Children

The game is meant for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. Ads are limited to content rated for general audiences.

Deleting your data

Your game progress lives on your device: uninstalling the game, or using Settings › Reset progress, deletes it. For anything else, email us.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
